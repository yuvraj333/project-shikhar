## What is Shikhar-AI?
Shikhar-AI is an autonomous multi-agent disaster response platform designed to triage chaotic, unstructured emergency reports (such as flash floods or landslides along mountain corridors like Bhote Koshi) into prioritized, actionable rescue directives.

### Key Features Highlighted in the Demo:
1. **Multimodal Incident Ingestion:** Instantly ingests raw, fragmented emergency reports from multiple communication channels.
2. **Autonomous Multi-Agent Pipeline (Gemini API):**
   - **Scout Agent:** Extracts critical incident facts, coordinates, and severity metrics.
   - **Logistics Agent:** Cross-references inventory and maps environmental hazards (e.g., blocked mountain roads).
   - **Communication Agent:** Synthesizes clear, prioritized rescue action plans in local languages (English & Nepali).
3. **Uncertainty & Fact Representation:** Flags missing or ambiguous data points for human review rather than making risky hallucinations.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop Safety:** Requires manual coordinator authorization before any final dispatch.
